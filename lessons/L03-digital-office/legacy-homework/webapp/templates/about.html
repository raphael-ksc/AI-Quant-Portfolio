跨資料源股票數據管線 & 多因子選股工具

問題背景

我是台灣居民，無法使用中國大陸券商的 QMT／miniQMT，這代表主流的 量化交易課程教材、社群範例，有很大一部分直接搬過來是行不通的。 這個專案的起點，是把一套「QMT 中心」的資料獲取邏輯，重新設計成 一套能在我實際可用的券商（Moomoo）與公開資料源（yfinance）上 運作的架構，同時保留原始邏輯裡值得參考的工程紀律 （模組化、防呆、風險檢查）。

關鍵設計決策

決策理由
資料源層獨立模組化（moomoo_loader / yfinance_loader） 券商與資料源會隨著市場、成本、監管而更換，抓資料邏輯需要能單獨替換，不影響上層的分析與呈現邏輯。
統一格式層強制標註幣別與資料源 跨市場比較最常見的錯誤是把不同幣別的數字直接放在一起看，這一層是防止這類低級錯誤的第一道防線。
因子計算（factor_engine）與資料抓取（screener）分離 因子計算是純函式（給定數據、算出比率），可以獨立寫單元測試、獨立驗證邏輯是否正確，不需要每次都連線券商才能測試。
用「近4季 vs 前4季」判斷趨勢，而非單季比較 資本支出常有明顯的季節性與一次性項目，單季比較容易被雜訊誤導，用季度區間平均值降低雜訊影響。

因子邏輯：為什麼看這三個 Capex 相關比率

這個篩選器要抓的不是「花很多錢做 AI 基礎建設」的公司，而是 「花錢做 AI 基礎建設、而且營運現金流已經開始有能力撐起這筆花費」 的公司——也就是資本支出循環從「故事」走向「現實」的轉折點：

  • Capex Intensity（資本支出 / 營收）：判斷公司是否真的在加碼擴產。
  • OCF Coverage of Capex（營運現金流 / 資本支出）：判斷這筆擴產花費是否已經被自身賺到的現金覆蓋，而非仰賴融資。這是最核心的「資金流開始 cover」指標。
  • FCF Margin（(營運現金流 − 資本支出) / 營收）：把前兩者合成一個最終指標，確認公司在擴產同時，是否仍然留得住現金。

已知限制（誠實揭露）

  • 倖存者偏差：板塊成分股是「現在」的名單，不包含已經下市或被移出板塊的公司，篩選結果會偏向目前仍存活、表現較好的公司。
  • 行情覆蓋範圍：Moomoo OpenAPI 目前不支援新加坡、日本、澳洲市場的行情數據，架構上必須為特定市場切換資料源。
  • 財報週期差異：非美股標的（如澳股）多為半年報制度，「近8季」的假設在這些市場不成立，需要自動降級為年報並明確標註。
  • 財務科目比對用關鍵字比對而非固定欄位 ID：Moomoo API 的欄位命名可能因版本而異，目前用字串比對辨識科目，屬於已知的脆弱點，未來可考慮改用官方 field_id 對照表加強穩健性。

Quant Watchlist Terminal

A cross-provider equity data pipeline & multi-factor screener

Background

As a Taiwan resident, I don't have access to mainland Chinese brokerage platforms such as QMT/miniQMT, which most mainstream quant trading course materials and community examples are built around. This project started as a re-architecture of a QMT-centric data retrieval workflow into one that runs on a broker I can actually use (Moomoo) and a public data source (yfinance), while keeping the engineering discipline worth carrying over — modularity, defensive design, and explicit risk checks.

Key design decisions

DecisionRationale
Independent, swappable data source modules (moomoo_loader / yfinance_loader) Brokers and data providers change with market coverage, cost and regulation, so the retrieval logic needs to be replaceable without touching the analysis or presentation layers above it.
A unified schema layer that enforces currency and source tagging The most common error in cross-market comparisons is plotting figures in different currencies on the same axis; this layer is the first line of defence against that mistake.
Factor computation (factor_engine) kept separate from data retrieval (screener) Factor computation is a pure function — given the data, it computes the ratios — which means it can be unit tested and validated independently, without needing a live broker connection for every test.
Trend judged via "last 4 quarters vs prior 4 quarters" rather than quarter-on-quarter Capex is often seasonal and lumpy; averaging across a rolling window reduces noise compared to a single-quarter comparison.

Factor logic: why these three capex-related ratios

The screener isn't looking for companies simply spending heavily on AI infrastructure — it's looking for companies whose operating cash flow has started to cover that spend. That's the inflection point where a capex cycle turns from narrative into fundamentals:

  • Capex intensity (capex / revenue): whether the company is genuinely ramping up build-out.
  • OCF coverage of capex (operating cash flow / capex): whether that build-out is already being funded by the company's own cash generation rather than external financing. This is the core signal for "cash flow starting to cover it."
  • FCF margin ((OCF − capex) / revenue): combines the two into a single check of whether the company is still generating cash while it expands.

Known limitations (disclosed honestly)

  • Survivorship bias: sector constituent lists reflect the present, excluding delisted or reclassified names, biasing results toward currently-surviving, better-performing companies.
  • Quote coverage gaps: the Moomoo OpenAPI currently doesn't support Singapore, Japanese or Australian market quotes, so the architecture has to switch data sources per market.
  • Reporting cadence differences: non-US tickers (e.g. Australian equities) typically report half-yearly rather than quarterly, so the "last 8 quarters" assumption doesn't hold there and the pipeline auto-falls-back to annual data with an explicit label.
  • Keyword-based line item matching: financial statement line items are matched by keyword rather than a stable field ID, since Moomoo's API field naming can vary by version — a known fragility that could be hardened with an official field ID lookup table.
